# Tiny

Short headings on purpose, so that both panels want much less than the role
share allows and there is room to watch them grow. The document still has to
clear `toc_minimum_length`, which is 1200 characters, before the table of
contents appears at all, so most of what follows is padding with that single
job.

## Aa

Body text under the first section. The width estimate for this document is
about 156 pixels for the outline and 123 for the table of contents at zoom
1.0, which leaves both of them well under the ceiling on a window this wide.

## Bb

More body text. Two characters is close to the narrow end of what the estimate
has to get right; the floor in `ROLE_MINIMUM` is what stops a group of
one-word headings collapsing to something nobody can grab with the mouse.

### Cc

A third level, so the outline has an indent to render and the table of
contents a nested entry. The indent counts towards the width, which is why a
deep heading with a short text can still be wider than a shallow one.

### Dd

The last section. Between them these five headings give the outline five
entries and the table of contents four, which is enough for both panels to
have a longest entry worth measuring.

Closing paragraph, so the file ends on prose rather than on a heading.
